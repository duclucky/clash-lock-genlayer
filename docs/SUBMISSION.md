## Exact How-to path

These five steps match the authenticated Portal draft. Fixed URLs are usable
directly. Braced IDs below mean the actual IDs generated by the application.

1. **Inspect the completed demo without a wallet.** Open
   https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/workspaces/billing-v1 and
   https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/requests/35fd50a0-97ba-4471-8c6e-5fa4fdc434c1.
   These are real historical records with returned deposits. Only registered
   participant wallets can write in existing workspaces.
2. **Connect and create your own workspace.** Open
   https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/workspaces/new. Select **Connect wallet**,
   choose OKX Wallet or MetaMask, and switch to Studionet when prompted. Fill
   **Workspace name**, **What does this resource contain?**, **Second participant
   wallet** and **Third participant wallet** using three distinct addresses,
   including your connected wallet. Select **Review workspace → Create with
   wallet**, approve the **0 GEN** call and wait for successful finality. Keep
   the generated workspace URL.
3. **Reserve a scope with exactly 1 GEN.** On that workspace select **Reserve
   work**, opening
   `https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/workspaces/{workspaceId}/request`.
   Use the real workspace ID from step 2. Enter a precise **Your work scope**
   within its resource, select **Exclusive work** and **10 minutes**, then
   **Review request → Sign 1 GEN request**. Approve exactly **1 GEN**. Successful
   finality opens
   `https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/requests/{requestId}` with your real ID.
4. **Review availability and return the deposit.** On that request select
   **Check availability** and approve **0 GEN**. Wait for successful finality
   and canonical **Reserved**, **Conflicting work** or **Clarify your scope**.
   When Reserved, optionally select **Record protocol action** (**0 GEN**, no
   external invoice execution), then **Release reservation** (**0 GEN**) before
   expiry. After expiry select **Recover expired deposit** (**0 GEN**). For
   your pending request select **Cancel & return deposit** (**0 GEN**). Wait for
   finality after each action.
5. **Withdraw your own credit and verify.** Open
   https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/account or select your wallet address
   **→ Wallet & deposits**. Confirm available credit, select **Withdraw to this
   wallet**, approve the **0 GEN** call and verify successful finality, credit
   and the explorer native transfer returning your credit in GEN. If
   **Confirmation unavailable** appears, inspect the existing hash and refresh;
   do not resend blindly. History is
   https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/activity and help is
   https://clash-lock-genlayer.vercel.app/help.
